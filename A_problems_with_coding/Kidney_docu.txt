Kidney transplant data
=========================================================================

This file contains data on 863 individuals who underwent a kidney transplant.

To load the data in R use the following command:
kidney <- read.csv("Kidney_data.csv", header=TRUE)

The variables are (in column order):

 1 ptid      patient id
 2 obstime   time-to-death or on-study time, days
 3 death     indicator of whether or not the observed time is a time-to-death; 0/1 = No/Yes
 4 gender    indicator of gender; 1/2 = male/female
 5 race      indicator of race; 1/2 = white/black
 6 age       age of the patient, in years
